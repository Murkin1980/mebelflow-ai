# Design System: MebelFlow AI — Voice Kitchen Architect
**Project ID:** 15036351447255177670

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
Quiet architectural luxury with the clarity of a professional interior studio. The interface combines an asymmetric editorial grid with an expressive voice-assistant state. It must feel crafted, precise and calm rather than like a generic SaaS dashboard. The kitchen scheme is the visual protagonist; controls frame it without competing for attention.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
- **Charcoal Ink (#121212):** primary actions, important typography and architectural dividers.
- **Warm Wood Gold (#C5A059):** the only expressive accent; microphone, active recording, focus and selected states.
- **Soft Porcelain (#FBF9F6):** main page background with a warm material quality.
- **Warm Stone (#EBE9E4):** secondary surfaces, transcript preview and scheme stage.
- **Graphite Grey (#707070):** supporting text and inactive metadata.
- **Deep Error Red (#9C3D31):** recording or validation failures only.
- **Quiet Sage (#657469):** confirmed, saved and successful states only.

## 3. Typography Rules
Use Newsreader for editorial display headings and important recognized speech. Its high-contrast serif character communicates an interior-design studio. Use Work Sans for body text, form controls, labels and operational status. Labels use uppercase with generous tracking; body copy remains compact and highly legible.

## 4. Component Stylings
* **Buttons:** Sharp, squared-off architecture. Primary send action uses Charcoal Ink. The microphone uses Warm Wood Gold and becomes an animated recording control with a pulse and waveform. Minimum touch target is 48 px.
* **Cards/Containers:** Avoid floating rounded cards. Use flat tonal sections, hairline dividers and asymmetric placement. Shadows are whisper-soft and reserved for the kitchen scheme.
* **Inputs/Forms:** Large editorial transcription surface with a clear baseline. Live speech appears simultaneously in a dedicated transcript panel and in the editable input. Focus uses Warm Wood Gold without a glow.
* **Voice State:** Always show explicit idle, listening, processing, transcript-ready and error labels. Listening uses a rhythmic waveform and recording dot. Never submit speech automatically.

## 5. Layout Principles
Desktop uses an asymmetric two-column magazine grid: conversation and voice controls on the left, large kitchen scheme on the right. Mobile uses one column with the conversation first and scheme second. Use generous whitespace for hierarchy, but keep the microphone and send controls immediately adjacent to the transcription surface. The main site link remains visible and understated.
