# Design System: MebelFlow AI — Voice Kitchen Architect
**Project ID:** 15036351447255177670

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
Quiet premium voice-assisted kitchen studio derived from the MebelDocs Unified visual language. The interface combines an asymmetric editorial layout with a precise professional workspace. Deep royal purple gives the product authority, muted gold brings furniture craftsmanship and warmth, while ivory and pale lilac surfaces keep the experience calm and approachable. The kitchen scheme remains the main visual object.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
- **Deep Royal Purple (#5B347F):** main brand color, microphone, primary controls and active states.
- **Shadow Purple (#431B67):** hover, pressed states and high-contrast structural areas.
- **Muted Furniture Gold (#C79A3B):** recording animation, progress, focus and carefully limited highlights.
- **Warm Archive Ivory (#F3E7D1):** atmospheric background and furniture-material warmth.
- **Pale Lilac Surface (#FFF7FF):** main page and content surfaces.
- **Soft Purple Container (#F6E9FE):** transcript preview and secondary sections.
- **Deep Aubergine Text (#1F1927):** main text and precise diagram labels.
- **Muted Purple Grey (#4B444F):** supporting text and inactive metadata.
- **Burgundy Error (#8B3A4A):** errors and blocked states only.
- **Quiet Teal (#16867A):** successful and confirmed states only.

## 3. Typography Rules
Use Manrope throughout, matching the established MebelDocs Unified design system. Large headlines use weight 700 with compact line height; section headings use weight 600. Body and operational copy use weights 400–500. Small labels use uppercase text with restrained tracking. Recognized speech must be large and easy to scan before submission.

## 4. Component Stylings
* **Buttons:** Soft 8 px corners. Primary send and microphone actions use Deep Royal Purple with white text. Recording changes the microphone emphasis to Muted Furniture Gold and adds a waveform plus pulse. Minimum touch target is 48 px.
* **Cards/Containers:** Pale lilac and ivory tonal layering, 8–16 px corners and very soft purple-tinted ambient shadows. Avoid generic card grids and excessive floating containers.
* **Inputs/Forms:** Light surface with a subtle purple-grey border. Focus uses Deep Royal Purple and a small gold detail. Live speech appears both in a dedicated transcript panel and in the editable input.
* **Voice State:** Always show explicit idle, listening, processing, transcript-ready and error labels. Listening uses a rhythmic gold waveform and recording dot. Speech is never submitted automatically.

## 5. Layout Principles
Desktop uses an asymmetric split view: conversation and voice controls on the left, a large kitchen scheme on the right. Mobile uses one column with the conversation first and scheme second. Keep microphone and send controls immediately adjacent to the transcription surface. Use an 8 px rhythm with purposeful whitespace. The main Salamat Mebel link remains visible but secondary.
